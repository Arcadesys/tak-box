# Physical acceptance — compact home board

All items are untested. Record printed source revision, material/profile,
actual felt/adhesive thickness and photos with each observed result.

- [ ] Front/rear grip coupons print cleanly and have comfortable contact edges.
- [ ] One cassette supports a representative fully loaded set without tab flex,
      cracking, an insecure grasp or dropped pieces.
- [ ] First/middle/last flats and the capstone body lift out comfortably.
- [ ] Both printed cassettes lower into the frame and release independently.
- [ ] Board contacts all support pads without rocking or loaded deflection.
- [ ] Board and loaded-cassette fingers reach the grip spaces comfortably.
- [ ] Repacking is repeatable and pieces stay supported during vertical motion.
- [ ] Quiet storage, enclosing retention and spill-free carrying are established
      after a separate retention design exists; the current CAD has no latch.
