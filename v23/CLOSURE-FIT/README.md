# Optional recessed-closure trial

Open [recessed-closure-fit-check-CC2-PLA.3mf](recessed-closure-fit-check-CC2-PLA.3mf). Estimated print: **52 min 55 s / 13.39 g**. All six trial parts print flat in the supplied arrangement. Keep the included build-plate-only supports and zero-brim object setting.

This contains actual cropped left/right housing walls, cropped board-edge strips, and the complete revised hook and round collar. Two extra rear pillars in each housing establish the 32.6 mm closed spacing; a rear ledge supports each cropped board edge. These fixture additions are not full-case features. Keep the cropped board strips in place when evaluating thumb access.

Fit one 8.6 mm length of straight 1.75 mm PLA as the pivot. Place the round collar against the hook; the pin starts 1 mm behind the outer collar face. Dry-fit before bonding the pin at the fixed mount and the collar to the pin. Keep glue off the moving hook. The hinge between the full bases is absent here: hold the two trial halves together against their alignment pillars, then turn the hook over the catch.

Check fingertip access, smooth 90° release/parking, catch engagement, reverse stop, useful pivot friction and collar retention. Stop if the thin sections crack or the motion binds. Record force, comfort, wear and snag observations. This trial does not qualify full-case warping, the main hinges, board catches, storage or loaded transport. Physical acceptance is unobserved.
