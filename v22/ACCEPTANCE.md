# V22 physical acceptance — unobserved

- [ ] Both captive main pivots release without cracks and fold smoothly through 180 degrees.
- [ ] Opposed pivots retain the halves after repeated opening and handling.
- [ ] Bases and boards remain flat enough for smooth sliding; both catches latch fully.
- [ ] Front buttons release comfortably without tools or excessive force.
- [ ] Full 5×5 field aligns across the centre seam and remains accessible with loaded stacks.
- [ ] Flat Cat and Witch capstones are distinguishable by outline and touch from ordinary stones and from each other.
- [ ] Capstones remain stable when used flat on the board and on stacks.
- [ ] Each capstone is easy to pinch and lift from its bay, including after any paint finish.
- [ ] All 42 original 20×20×8 mm flats and both supplied V22 flat capstones fit without rubbing the sliding boards.
- [ ] The single hook filament pivot and collar remain retained; hook friction resists accidental opening after wear.
- [ ] Loaded opening and repacking are spill-free with the case kept flat and boards latched before folding.
- [ ] Closed loaded transport does not release pieces or open the case.

The target is the original Cat/Witch ordinary flat stones plus the new V22 flat capstones. Older sculpted/curled capstones and other weighted-stone packages are incompatible or unqualified. No physical acceptance is inferred from CAD, slices or prior clasp/filament trials.
