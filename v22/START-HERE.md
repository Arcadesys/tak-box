# V22 — hinge candidate; capstone direction pending

**The box stays 32.6 mm thick. This is not the final V22 print release.** The user is choosing between one-piece flat capstones, folding standees, or the existing sculpted capstones with their rear compartment. Current full-size candidate files retain that existing compartment. No deeper-box geometry remains in the source or models.

The two bases now print together, open flat, with captive main pivots. Do not separate, arrange or reorient their parts in the slicer. Boards still slide outward and print separately. Main filament pins and their collars are removed; the current hatch and side hook retain their 1.75 mm filament pivots.

The independently useful [captive-hinge trial](FIT-CHECK/README.md) takes about 22 minutes / 3.8 g. Its selected layers and sampled running gaps have been checked digitally. Physical release and durability remain untested.

Current full-size candidate: three CC2 projects in PRINT, estimated at 12 h 9 min 58 s / 283.80 g. These are provisional until capstone storage is settled. No final ZIP has been generated and no printer job has been started.

Source and dependencies are included. See [build notes](BUILD-PROVENANCE.md) and [acceptance](ACCEPTANCE.md). The earlier V21 kit remains unchanged.
