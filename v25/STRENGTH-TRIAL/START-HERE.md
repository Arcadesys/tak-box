# V25: try the supported filament hinge

**Next action: print these eight small parts, then form both filament ends with your heated tool.** Start with S2. This trial targets a firm hinge that stays together; it is not yet a complete case or a proven durable hinge.

[Open the Centauri Carbon 2 PLA print plate](PRINT/01-V25-strength-hinges-CC2-PLA.3mf). **Slice estimate: 1 hour 34 minutes and 17.75 g of PLA.** Actual print time and use can differ. The plate contains S1, S2 and S3 fixed/moving pairs, plus two rounded underside board samples. The axle and its heads are ordinary 1.75 mm filament, not printed parts. Use the existing sliced PLA settings only with the matching material; changed material profiles need reslicing.

## Assemble S2

1. Clean the supports without thinning the roots or scraping inside the bearing bores. The **FIXED** half has two knuckles. The **MOVING** half fits between them.
2. Rehearse on scrap filament with your heated tool. Make a round head about **3.2 mm across and 0.6 mm thick**. Let it cool fully. The head must remain wider than the 1.95 mm fixed bore and fit inside the 3.5 mm recess.
3. Start with about **12 mm of straight ordinary PLA filament**. Form the first head, cool it, interleave the hinge halves and insert the filament through all three bores. Seat the first head in its recess.
4. Leave about **2 mm of forming stock beyond the far recess's inner shoulder** (about 1.3 mm beyond its outer opening). Trim if needed. Use your heated tool to form the second head inside the far recess. Use the tool's material guidance and your scrap trial to establish the heat setting; no temperature has been validated here.
5. Let both heads cool fully. Keep a small running allowance; do not clamp the fixed shoulders tightly together or melt the printed barrel. The nominal CAD allowance is 0.1 mm at each head. Both heads stay with the fixed half; the middle knuckle must rotate freely.
6. Inspect both heads and roots. Reject cracks, scorched/deformed journals, undersized heads, migration, binding or growing play. Use no glue to conceal an unsuccessful head. A single head will not retain this through-axle in both directions.

Try S1 if S2 rocks; try S3 if S2 binds. **S1 / S2 / S3 moving bores are 1.95 / 2.10 / 2.25 mm.** Fixed bores stay 1.95 mm. Record any reaming or other adjustment rather than silently changing the fit comparison.

## Check whether it stays together

Record the filament, print material, tool setting and head dimensions. Compare initial fit, 20 cycles and 100 cycles. At each point, check side-to-side rocking, gentle twisting and axial pulling in both directions. Record actual force if measured; no load rating is claimed. Recheck after resting overnight.

Reject axle escape, growing play, head separation, root cracks or fractured barrels. Then test the two board samples through the same folding range. Coupon success is only the first gate: the selected hinge still needs integration into the whole case and a loaded opening/repacking and transport trial.
