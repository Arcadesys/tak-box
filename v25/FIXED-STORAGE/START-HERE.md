# V25: fixed wells, no removable trays

**Open the covers and tip the pieces onto the table.** V25 now has one plain storage well per side. There are no lift-out trays, tray handles or individual piece pockets.

The integral case floor is **3.4 mm**, replacing the former 2.2 mm case floor plus 1.2 mm tray floor. The outside size stays **102.5 × 200 × 35 mm**, and the 180 mm playing field stays unchanged. The body uses B's middle filament fit at both ends of the hinge. This is a complete-body prototype, not a physically qualified print release.

## Next useful trial

First try the [reinforced B coupon](../REINFORCED-B-TRIAL/START-HERE.md): approximately 52 minutes / 10.29 g PLA, with 6 mm sample floors. This isolates the hinge from the thin-body failure already reported. B is the selected fit assumption, not a durability pass.

[The new two-body CC2 PLA plate](PRINT/01-V25-fixed-storage-bodies-CC2-PLA.3mf) makes **two replacement case bodies**. Slice estimate: **8 hours 23 minutes / 153.30 g PLA**; actual time and use can differ. Both must be replaced as a pair. The current V24 four-colour sliding covers and side hook are retained as reference geometry and reuse candidates; no new covers, pieces, hook or tray are printed by this plate. Whole-case physical fit remains open. No printer job has been started.

The body has two opposed-head filament axles: front and rear. Use ordinary 1.75 mm PLA filament and the heated tool to form both heads of each axle. Each reference axle is 9 mm long after forming; approximately 12 mm unformed stock is the starting length. Fixed bores are 1.95 mm, moving bores 2.10 mm; nominal heads are 3.2 mm across × 0.6 mm thick inside 3.5 mm recesses. Rehearse on scrap and leave running allowance. The [B guide](../REINFORCED-B-TRIAL/START-HERE.md) describes forming and inspection.

## Pack and open

Store the **42 original Cat/Witch 20 × 20 × 8 mm flats and two supplied 8 mm V23 flat capstones**, 21 flats and one capstone per side. Weighted and sculpted packages have not been checked. Spread the pieces flat in one layer before sliding the covers shut; do not force a cover over overlapping pieces.

Open the case on the table, release and slide off the covers, then tip the contents out. Refit the covers for play. Pieces are loose in the wells and can move or rattle; random packing, dumping, closed shake/drop/carry retention and repeated repacking still need physical observation.

The thin V24 cover catch, broad-lip revision and selected rounded-cover treatment are separate from this tray-removal change. This prototype preserves current V24 covers rather than claiming those pending mechanisms are integrated. The side hook remains the closure backup.

The PLA profile retains ElegooSlicer's inherited bed-temperature warning. Confirm the actual spool/profile and reslice if changed. [Engineering record](ENGINEERING.md).
