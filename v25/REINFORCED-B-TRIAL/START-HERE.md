# V25: reinforced B sample

**Use B as the selected middle fit.** The user reports that the thin sample bodies fell apart. B is the working fit assumption; hinge durability has not passed a physical test.

[Open the four-part CC2 PLA trial plate](PRINT/01-V25-reinforced-B-CC2-PLA.3mf). It contains one B fixed/moving pair and two rounded underside board samples. The fixture floors are now **6 mm thick**, compared with 2.2 mm before. The added 3.8 mm is below the reference case floor. This is sample reinforcement, not a change to the whole case.

The B moving bore stays **2.10 mm** for ordinary **1.75 mm filament**. Fixed bores stay 1.95 mm. The bearing dimensions, two-sided support and recessed formed-head geometry are unchanged. Four-millimetre-wide continuous fixture spines connect the front and rear portions of each sample body. B is engraved on the sample floors.

## Assemble and test

1. Remove supports in small pieces while supporting the sample body. Keep the bearing bores unchanged for this comparison.
2. With your heated tool, form one filament head on approximately 12 mm of straight ordinary PLA filament. Aim for a 3.2 mm diameter and 0.6 mm thickness; rehearse on scrap and cool fully.
3. Interleave the two fixed knuckles around the middle moving knuckle. Insert the filament and seat its first head in the recess.
4. Leave about 2 mm of forming stock beyond the far recess's inner shoulder, approximately 1.3 mm beyond the outer opening. Form the second head inside the recess, then cool fully. Keep a small running allowance rather than squeezing the shoulders together. Tool heat and forming quality remain unqualified.
5. Check that the middle knuckle rotates freely. Reject damaged roots, cracked or undersized heads, a melted journal, or axle migration. Both heads are necessary to retain this through-axle in both directions.
6. Compare initial movement with 20 and 100 cycles, gentle rocking/twisting and axial pulls in both directions. Record actual force if measured. Recheck overnight. Stop if the body, hinge or head begins cracking; identify the failed component separately.
7. Test the two board samples through the same range. A successful reinforced coupon still needs whole-case integration and loaded opening/repacking tests.

**Slice estimate: about 52 minutes / 10.29 g PLA.** Actual time and use can differ. The supplied slice uses ordinary PLA, 0.20 mm layers, four walls and supports. **ElegooSlicer retains the inherited bed-temperature warning** (60°C textured-bed profile / 45°C vitrification metadata). Confirm the profile matches the actual spool before printing; reslice if profiles change. No printer job has been started.
