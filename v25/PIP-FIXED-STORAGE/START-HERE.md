# V25: captive PIP hinges and fixed wells

**Print the two halves together.** The main hinge now uses opposed captive **4 mm PIP pivots**, with **0.4 mm radial and face clearance** and full-height **8 mm roots** directly attached to the case bodies. No inserted pin or heat-formed filament ends are required for the main hinge. The retained side hook still uses its small filament pivot.

The two plain wells have **3.4 mm integral floors**. There are no removable trays or dividers. The full 180 mm / 36 mm-cell playing field and **102.5 × 200 × 35 mm** closed dimensions stay unchanged. The thicker fixed floor may improve body stiffness; physical hinge strength and fatigue remain unverified.

## Start with the hinge trial

[Full-length hinge trial](PRINT/02-V25-PIP-hinge-trial-CC2-PLA.3mf) estimates **2 hours 59 minutes / 46.79 g PLA** and prints both opposed pivot stations and a continuous wide spine cut from the actual production bodies. It retains the production 3.4 mm floor, 200 mm hinge length and exact bearing geometry. This avoids testing an isolated thin coupon body or a single uncaptured pivot. Both halves must remain together as **one assembly**. Do not auto-arrange or separate its components.

Let the print cool, remove accessible external supports and gently free the joint. Reject a fused bearing, a crack or a broken root; do not force it. Record whether each pivot releases and how much play it has. Check both axial pull directions and gentle twisting, then repeat at 20 and 100 open/close cycles and after resting overnight. Record material, slicer profile and observed failures. No strength rating is inferred from this test.

## Complete bodies after the trial

[Full replacement-body assembly](PRINT/01-V25-PIP-fixed-storage-CC2-PLA.3mf) estimates **8 hours 13 minutes / 153.39 g PLA** and prints two new case bodies together, open flat. Replace both as a pair; these bodies do not mate with the earlier filament-hinge bodies. Reusing the current V24 sliding covers and side hook is a candidate pending physical fit. This plate makes no covers, hook, pieces or trays. Do not separate or independently arrange the two PIP body components.

The thin V24 cover catch, broad-lip revision and selected rounded-cover underside are still pending complete-case integration. The current exact V24 four-colour cover STEP references are retained. PIP is the selected hinge direction; B's filament fit is historical and does not establish this pivot clearance.

## Pack and open

Use the **42 original Cat/Witch 20 × 20 × 8 mm flats and two supplied 8 mm V23 flat capstones**: 21 flats and one capstone per well. Weighted and sculpted pieces have not been checked. Spread loose pieces flat in one layer before closing. Open on the table, slide off the covers and tip the contents out, then refit the covers for play. Pieces may shift or rattle. Dumping, repeated repacking, cover retention and loaded transport still need physical observation.

The earlier strong PIP version the user remembers has not been identified by a physical test record. The restored source uses the V16-style opposed pivots retained in V24, with the reinforced V24 roots. CAD capture and sampled slicer gaps do not establish physical release or durability.

The PLA project retains the inherited ElegooSlicer bed-temperature warning (60°C textured-bed setting / 45°C vitrification metadata). Match the actual spool and reslice if settings change. No printer job has been started. [Engineering record](ENGINEERING.md).
