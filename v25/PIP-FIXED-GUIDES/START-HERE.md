# V25: PIP hinges with fixed piece guides

**This is the latest fixed-guide body plate.** Each case half has **21 flat-stone positions and one capstone position**, permanently joined to the **3.4 mm case floor**. No removable tray or tray handle is printed. The older empty-well plate is preserved separately.

The guides use the earlier tray's layout and broad grasp openings. They stand **3.5 mm above the floor**, with tops at Z6.9 mm; an 8 mm stone projects **4.5 mm above them** for grasping. Nominal-piece CAD checks verify seating, straight upward removal, covered lateral restraint and rear fingertip access. Physical fit and dumping still need a print test.

The main hinge remains opposed captive **4 mm PIP pivots**, **0.4 mm radial/face clearance** and full-height **8 mm roots**. Both halves must print together as **one assembly**. Open each 3MF as a project in ElegooSlicer; do not auto-arrange or separate its components. No inserted axle is required for the main hinge; the retained side hook still uses its small filament pivot.

## Print projects

[Complete bodies with fixed guides](PRINT/01-V25-PIP-fixed-guides-CC2-PLA.3mf) estimates **9 hours 21 minutes / 163.69 g PLA** and prints two replacement bodies together, open flat. Both must be replaced as a pair. It prints no covers, hook, pieces or removable trays. The current V24 four-colour covers and side hook are reference/reuse candidates pending physical fit. Outside dimensions remain **102.5 × 200 × 35 mm**, with the same full 180 mm field and independent accent colours.

[Full-length PIP hinge trial](PRINT/02-V25-PIP-guided-hinge-trial-CC2-PLA.3mf) estimates **3 hours 7 minutes / 47.87 g PLA** and retains both opposed pivots, the continuous production-thickness spine and guide fragments from the actual bodies. It isolates hinge release and retention before the complete-body print; it does not test every stone pocket. Both components remain one assembly.

Let the print cool, remove accessible external supports and gently free the joint. Reject fusion, cracks or broken roots. Record play, both-direction axial pull and gentle twisting, then recheck after 20/100 cycles and overnight. Test each nominal stone position and capstone guide on the complete bodies. Do not force a tight fit. Cover fit, strength and loaded transport remain physical gates. No printer job has been started.

## Pack and empty

Use **42 original Cat/Witch 20 × 20 × 8 mm flats and two supplied 8 mm V23 flat capstones**, 21 flats and one capstone per side. Weighted/sculpted packages are unqualified. Place pieces individually in their guides in one layer, then slide the covers shut. Open on the table, slide off the covers and tip the pieces out. Keep a hand or the table ready to catch them. Dumping and repeated repacking remain untested physical actions.

The thin V24 cover catch, broad-lip change and selected rounded-cover underside still need complete-case integration. This revision adds fixed piece guides; its complete covers remain current V24 references. The remembered physically strong PIP version is unidentified; restored captive geometry and digital checks do not prove durability.

The PLA project retains the inherited bed-temperature warning (60°C textured bed / 45°C vitrification metadata). Match the actual spool/profile and reslice if settings change. [Engineering record](ENGINEERING.md).
