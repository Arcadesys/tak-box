# Optional V21 board-slider fit check

The full four-plate build is already in ../PRINT. This smaller print tests the new rail and front-button joint.

Open **rail-and-catch-fit-check-CC2-PLA.3mf**. Estimate: **60 min / 19 g**, including brims/supports. Both pieces print directly on their broad undersides.

The fixture uses X=0..70 mm sections of the actual left base and board. The rear sections move 160 mm forward and are joined with short bridges; the board gets a small fixture pull tab. Rail cross-sections, catch dimensions and their vertical positions remain unchanged. The fixture does not test full-board warping, inner stops, hinges, side hook, loading or transport.

Remove brim and local support. Insert the board from the open side, pressing its front button toward the rear. Align the button with its window, release it and check that a gentle outward pull is held. Press about 2.8 mm inward to release, keeping the button pressed until it passes out of the rail. Check smooth travel, vertical retention and repeated comfortable release without whitening or cracks. The fixture has no inner end stop and can be pushed through; its parts are not used in the full assembly.

No physical test result is recorded yet.
