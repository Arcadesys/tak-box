# V21 physical acceptance — unobserved

User reports an earlier clasp trial and a 1.75 mm PLA filament hinge trial. Those do not establish V21 board fit, pin strength, force, wear or loaded retention. Record component/project hashes, spool settings, photos and measured results; leave unobserved items unchecked.

- [ ] Bases, rail edges, board flex slots and hinge bores print cleanly; supports and brim remove without damage.
- [ ] Each board enters its rails smoothly, reaches its inner stops and releases with comfortable 2.8 mm button travel.
- [ ] Each button springs back, holds an outward pull and remains accessible without tools.
- [ ] Both boards resist lifting, stay level and do not slide or rock under ordinary play.
- [ ] All five rows and columns align across the centre seam; the full board is comfortable to use when loaded.
- [ ] Boards can be removed and replaced without rubbing loaded flats, dropping pieces or catching the parked hook.
- [ ] Every original flat fits its seat and can be grasped individually.
- [ ] The two bases rotate freely on retained filament pins; glue stays out of moving joints.
- [ ] Main pins are the V21 14.9 mm length; collars remain attached and clear the moving barrels.
- [ ] Capstones can be retrieved comfortably; the hatch latches and stays closed during transport.
- [ ] The side hook seats over its keeper and operates comfortably from the front corner.
- [ ] Hook friction remains sufficient after repeated cycles; carrying/contact does not rotate it open.
- [ ] Loaded unfold/open/repack sequence retains every piece until deliberately exposed with the case flat.
- [ ] Loaded shake, inversion and carry checks retain all pieces without objectionable rattle.
- [ ] Repeated use shows no cracks, whitening, permanent spring set, rail wear or binding.
- [ ] Actual envelope, pin play, board alignment and release effort are recorded.

Stop for forced assembly, damaged flexures, binding or unexpected release. Digital collision checks and sliced files are not physical acceptance.
