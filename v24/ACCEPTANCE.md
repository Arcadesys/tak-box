# V24 physical acceptance — pending

Observed baseline: the user reports that the V23 print broke at the thin bar carrying the main hinge. The fracture location, fused-joint state and action causing failure are not recorded. No V24 physical result has been observed.

- [ ] Small hinge trial: support removal, joint release and full folding without whitening, cracking or binding. Record which joint and material/profile if it fails.
- [ ] Small tray trial: actual pieces fit; handle can be comfortably pinched; loaded section lifts and reseats without catching.
- [ ] Complete paired bases: flat bed contact and field alignment; repeated loaded opening/closing without cracks or excessive play.
- [ ] Full trays: stable on the table, acceptable flex with 21 flats and capstone, no warping that catches rails or rubs covers; comfortable loaded removal/repacking.
- [ ] Four-colour surface trial with actual silk profiles: flush top, bonded inlays, readable white grid, continuous fine accents and acceptable colour bleed. Record all four materials, purge and temperatures.
- [ ] Both board catches latch and release comfortably; both sliders travel freely over full loaded trays.
- [ ] Hook remains closed during loaded inversion and gentle shake; no pieces escape or trays leave their seats during controlled opening/repacking. Assess rattle.
- [ ] Representative padded carry/handling and repeated cycles, followed by inspection of hinge roots, barrels, floors and hook. Record conditions and results; do not infer purse durability from CAD or the small trial.

Trials are candidates for testing. No measured force, fatigue life, drop resistance, printed fit or universal filament compatibility is claimed. Actual PLA brand, printer calibration and handling can affect results.
