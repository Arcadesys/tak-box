# Print V26

Open the three projects in **FULL-PRINT**. The board tabs are gone: open the case hook, then pull each board straight out. A solid seating shoulder sets each board's inward position without a press-and-pull action.

1. [PIP case bodies, fixed guides and captive hook](FULL-PRINT/01-V26-PIP-guides-and-hook-CC2-PLA.3mf). Both bodies and the hook print together as one assembly. Keep their placement; do not separate or auto-arrange the captive parts. **9h 28m / 164.56 g**.
2. [FULL-SIZE four-colour board pair](FULL-PRINT/02-V26-FULL-SIZE-FOUR-COLOUR-tab-free-boards-CC2-PLA.3mf). Two complete playing surfaces, each grouped with its inlays. Keep the five parts of each board registered together. **8h 1m / 121.19 g**.
3. [Flat Cat and Witch capstones](FULL-PRINT/03-V26-flat-capstones-CC2-PLA.3mf). Optional replacements; reuse the supplied flat capstones if you already have them. Reuse the original 42 Cat/Witch 20 × 20 × 8 mm flats. **16m 30s / 3.78 g**.

Total estimate: **17h 45m / 289.53 g PLA**, including the optional capstones. Estimates change when resliced.

These are Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2 / 0.4 mm nozzle projects. Case and capstones use 0.20 mm layers; boards use 0.10 mm layers with six 0.6 mm inlay layers. The board slots are **1 black body, 2 white grid and stars, 3 orange accent, 4 purple accent**. The two silk accent profiles are labelled generic PLA placeholders. Select the actual spool profiles and reslice when materials or settings change. Current slicing retains the inherited textured-bed metadata warning in the reports.

Let the parts cool, remove accessible external support, and gently free the main hinges and captive hook. Check for cracks, fusion and excessive looseness before loading pieces. No inserted hinge pins, hook pin, collar, removable tray or loose guide parts are required.

Lay the opened case flat with its hook parked at 90°. Slide both boards inward to their solid seating stops and align the centre seam. For storage access, pull the boards outward; fully removed covers allow the pieces to be dumped at the table. Before closing, reload the fixed guides, seat both covers, fold the case and close the hook. **The hook secures the covers during closed transport; opening it lets the boards slide freely.**

The user elected to proceed to the full plates before physical trials. CAD and slicing checks establish nominal geometry and toolpaths; hinge strength, hook retention, handling comfort, dumping and loaded transport remain untested physically. The earlier V17-style rounded underside request remains outside this plate revision.
